# Security Architecture Review Checklist

## Business Context

- What problem is being solved?
- What are the critical business assets?
- What is the organization's risk tolerance?

## Identity and Access

- Authentication mechanism defined?
- Authorization model documented?
- Privileged access identified?
- Service accounts reviewed?

## Data Protection

- Data classification completed?
- Encryption requirements defined?
- Data retention requirements documented?

## Network Security

- Trust boundaries identified?
- Segmentation requirements documented?
- External connectivity reviewed?

## Logging and Monitoring

- Security logging defined?
- Monitoring requirements documented?
- Incident response considerations included?

## Resilience

- Backup requirements defined?
- Recovery objectives documented?
- Availability requirements identified?

## Final Questions

- What are the highest risks?
- What controls mitigate those risks?
- What residual risks remain?
- Has risk ownership been assigned?
